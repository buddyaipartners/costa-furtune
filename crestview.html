02 Monolithic Engineering

One towering presence.
Twelve floors of resolve.

Crestview is the flagship high-rise phase of Costa Furtune — an uninterrupted B+S+12 residential tower holding 144 three-bedroom apartments and two ground-floor essential convenience stores.

Structural engineering is executed by SR Engineering to Seismic Zone III Heavy RCC frame specifications; aesthetic architecture by Vogue Architect (Shivangi Pradhan). Six high-speed passenger elevators (OTIS / KONE) with Automatic Rescue Devices (ARD) serve the tower, connected via a dedicated 15.25 m external arterial road with 2,078.55 sqm of dedicated landscaped perimeter.
