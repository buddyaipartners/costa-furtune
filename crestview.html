01 The Architecture

One tower.
Twelve floors of resolve.

Crestview is the only high-rise phase of the Costa Furtune master township — a single, uninterrupted B+S+12 residential tower holding 144 flats and two ground-floor shops.

Structural design is by SR Engineering to Seismic Zone III RCC frame specification; architecture is by Vogue Architect. Six passenger lifts — KONE, ECE or OTIS, 6-person capacity with automatic rescue device — serve the tower, with a dedicated 15.25 m external road and 2,078.55 sqm of site development.
