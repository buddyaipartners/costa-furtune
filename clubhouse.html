01 The Vertical Club

Four floors.
One social life.

Club Costa Fantasy is deliberately vertical. Instead of spreading amenities across the site, the club stacks them into a single G+4 volume so that everything is a short walk from everything else.

It serves Crestview, the Triplex villas and Horizon Heights together — which means the club stays busy through every phase of the township's life, not just the one that happens to be selling. That is also why it is the part of the master plan least exposed to how any single phase performs.
