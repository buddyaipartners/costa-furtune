01 The Vertical Club

Three floors & Sky Terrace.
One social life.

Club Costa Fantasy is structured as a dedicated G+3 lifestyle volume crowned by a 4th floor open-to-sky party terrace. Instead of spreading amenities across the site, the club integrates wellness, games, banqueting, and open-air aquatic leisure into a unified vertical volume.

It serves Crestview, the Triplex villas and Horizon Heights together — which means the club stays vibrant through every phase of the township's life. Civil structure is already completed on site.
