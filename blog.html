02 How to Read a Pre-Launch

Four questions
that settle most arguments.

Ask these before you visit any site in the corridor — including this one.

i The Registration

Is the phase registered,
or only announced?

An ORERA number is a filing with declared carpet areas, balcony areas, unit counts and a possession date. A brochure without one is a rendering with a phone number on it. Crestview is MP/19/2024/01244; the Triplex villas are RP/19/2023/00854; Horizon Heights is RP/19/2026/01487.

ii The Area Basis

Carpet, built-up,
or super built-up?

The three can differ by 30% on the same flat. A 1,845 sq ft saleable area on Crestview is 1,016.5 sq ft of RERA carpet. Compare like with like or the price per square foot is fiction.

iii The Possession

A date, or
a quarter?

“2028” is not a commitment. Crestview's registered date is 19 August 2028, which is a specific day the promoter can be held to. Ask every project in the corridor for the exact date on the registration.

iv The Delivery Record

Has this developer
finished anything?

Phase 1 is the answer here: a delivered, occupied villa enclave you can walk through before you buy into Phase 2. A completed phase is the only track record that cannot be rendered.
